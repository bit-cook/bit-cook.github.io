BitCook is an ever-changing personal digital workshop. It explores how AI can become a creative partner, and how open technology can help individuals preserve memory, extend capability, and own their tools.

Every project is a runnable version of a question: Can agents become as dependable as operating systems? Can programs write themselves? How can humans and machines collaborate better in the real world?
